# Puzzles

A jigsaw puzzle app for Mom's phone or tablet. She can make a puzzle from her own photos (her flowers, her
finished cross-stitch) or pick one of the built-in pictures. It has big buttons and saves her place
automatically, and it works without internet once it's installed.

## Try it on this computer

```bash
python -m http.server 8123
```

Then open http://localhost:8123.

## Put it on her phone

The app has to be hosted on an `https://` address first. Either of these is free:

- **Netlify Drop**: go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole folder onto the page.
- **GitHub Pages**: push this folder to a GitHub repository, then turn on Pages under Settings → Pages.

Then, on her phone, open the address and add it to the home screen:

- **iPhone / iPad (Safari)**: tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome)**: tap ⋮, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

It then opens full screen like a normal app. Her photos and progress are kept on her device only.

## Making changes

- Built-in pictures are drawn in code in `js/pictures.js`.
- The puzzle engine (piece shapes, dragging, snapping) is in `js/puzzle.js`.
- After changing any file, bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` (e.g. `puzzles-v2`) so her phone picks up the new version.
- Icons are generated by `python tools/make_icons.py`.
